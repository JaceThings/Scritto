Roll

Anything you can type rolls in place. Click the card for the next value, or edit the list below to roll your own. A comma or Enter starts a new one.

Values
Comma or Enter splits at the caret. Commas are drawn, never typed. Backspace at the start of a value joins it to the one before.

Alignment

Which side the value holds when it gets wider or narrower. Dynamic slides the number back and forth so you can watch the roll hold together while it moves.

Trend

Which way the glyphs travel. Auto reads the numbers and follows them; up and down override it.

Bounce

How far each character overshoots as it lands. Zero settles flat; the library ships at 0.20 and its bouncy preset is 0.30.

Duration

How long the roll takes end to end. Drag the track, or focus it and use the arrow keys.

In a sentence

A live number sitting in ordinary copy. Words on either side make room as the figure grows or shrinks.

Light has traveled km since this minute began.

Wrapped

When the value changes width, the words after it slide between lines and the block stays exactly as tall as it was. All at the value's own duration.
