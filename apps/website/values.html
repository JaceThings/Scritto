scritto

/ˈskrit.to/ adj. & n. [It., pp. of scrivere, to write] Pronounced skreet-toh. Adjective and noun, from Italian, past participle of scrivere, to write.

1 a written; set down in letters rather than spoken (un testo scritto).

b fig. settled in advance, as though already written (era scritto).

2 n. a piece of writing; a text as it stands on the page (gli scritti di Calvino).
